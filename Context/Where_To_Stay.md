# Haarlem
The wedding location is only 5 km away of the city center of Haarlem. Haarlem is a culturally rich and historic town with typical Dutch architecture. It can be reached from Airport Schiphol in 25 minutes by train.

## Tips
- Frans Hals museum
- Visit the market on the Grote Markt on Saturday morning

# Zandvoort
If you go 5 km into the other direction from the wedding location you find the coastal city Zandvoort. It is not famous for it's historic architecture like Haarlem, but the city is directly on the beach. Zandvoort is also famous for the Formula 1 circuit.

# Center Parcs - Park Zandvoort
Best price for groups. Park Zandvoort is about 15 minutes from the wedding location by car, 25 minutes by bike, or 1 hour and 30 minutes on foot. It offers detached cottages, e.g. for three to six people with three beds. It's not listed on Booking.com, and appears to be one of the least expensive options when shared with a group. Stays seem to be available for three nights only, for example from Friday to Monday for 5 guests is currently 900 EUR. If you would like to stay here but do not have a group, let us know and we can try to form one.
